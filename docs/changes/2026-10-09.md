## Web: /routing driven against a live device and a real broker

Host on the DG1062Z, Mosquitto from `containers/` on 127.0.0.1:1883. In headless Edge, `/routing` got host `127.0.0.1`, one Device-to-broker rule (`^(?<m>.+)$` to `bench/model`, payload `${m}`) and Apply (status `Broker: Connected`). `*IDN?` sent over `/ws` produced the MQTT message `bench/model Rigol Technologies,DG1062Z,DG1ZA232603118,03.01.12` (seen with `mosquitto_sub`), and the page's history row and `Unmatched: 0` agreed. A first attempt matched nothing only because its regex said `RIGOL` and the reply is `Rigol` (a counted unmatched line, as designed). Rule edits are committed on change, so a script must blur a field before Apply; a real click does that itself. Throwaway probe test, not kept.
