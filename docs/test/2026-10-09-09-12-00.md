## Findings

- The DS1ET gave no reply to `*IDN?` under the `ascii` presenter. It matches the known terminatorless DS1000 behaviour (an AsciiPresenter never flushes a reply without a terminator); a `raw` presenter pass is still to do.
- The DG1062Z and DG1000Z entries in the USB list are the same unit as the LAN one (serial DG1ZA232603118), reachable over both USBTMC and VXI-11.
- The USB list shows the DG1022 as "DG3000 SERIES" (its USB descriptor string). It and the DS1000 share PID 0588; `--serialnumber` disambiguates.
- The DG1022 does not answer `:SYSTem:ERRor?` (no SCPI error-queue query on this older model); the USBTMC transport reports the timeout, drops the connection and reconnects on the next line, as designed.
