## Findings

- Both 2230s are online at once now (.107 VERS:14, .108 VERS:13), so the docs' "usually only one is online" note is
  out of date for today's bench.
- The DG1022 and DS1102E share VID:PID `1AB1:0588`; selecting by `--serialnumber` told them apart.
- The Korad units send no terminator; `ascii` would never flush, so `hex` was used.
- Nothing beyond the identify was sent to the Rigol units; their profiles are untested this session.
- The TDS2024 was left in the state set earlier for the handshake capture (CH3/CH4 5 V/div, 1 s/div, trigger CH3).
